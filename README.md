## 👤 About Me

I am a driven and ambitious STEM professional with a strong commitment to excellence, innovation, and leadership. I thrive in dynamic environments where I can apply engineering principles and data-driven techniques to solve real-world problems and create meaningful impact.

My core passion lies at the intersection of **Civil Engineering, Data Science, and Machine Learning**, particularly in the analysis of materials and concrete structures for reliable prediction and informed engineering decision-making.

This vision is reflected in my **[ML4CivilEngineers](https://github.com/AbubakarDanhaya/ML4CivilEngineers)** journey, which documents my transition from Python fundamentals to data-driven and research-oriented machine learning applications, with emphasis on **interpretable solutions for structural health monitoring and infrastructure assessment**.
